Projects

Here are two projects I've worked on during my electrical engineering studies.

Common-Emitter Amplifier

I designed, built, and tested a BJT common-emitter amplifier.

Technologies: BJT circuits, PSpice, oscilloscope

The design called for a gain of at least 100 and a voltage swing of at least ±3.5 V. I modeled the circuit in PSpice, then measured its gain and swing in the lab.

Pipelined RISC-V Processor

I worked with a team to design a five-stage pipelined RISC-V processor.

Technologies: Verilog, RISC-V, digital design

I developed a forwarding unit to pass results between stages and a hazard detection unit to stall the pipeline when needed.
